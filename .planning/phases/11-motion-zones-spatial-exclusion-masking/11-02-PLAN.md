<threat_model>
### ASVS L1 Threat Model: Motion Zones UI
- **T-11-01 (Client-Side Out-of-Bounds Vertex Injection)**: Malformed or negative coordinates generated by dragging outside canvas boundaries.
  - *Mitigation*: Client clamps all vertex coordinates strictly between `0.0` and `1.0`.
- **T-11-02 (Unauthorized Configuration Exposure)**: Viewers or operators seeing zone modification controls.
  - *Mitigation*: Zone editing modal and tile button are visible only to users with `role === 'ADMIN'`.
</threat_model>
